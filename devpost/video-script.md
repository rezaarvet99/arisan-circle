# Video Demo Script (English, ~75 seconds)

1. Hi! This is Arisan Circle, a one-page tracker for Indonesian rotating savings groups.
2. The problem: an arisan treasurer tracks who paid and whose turn it is in a paper book, and easily forgets.
3. Here is the running app. Five members, dues one hundred thousand rupiah each, round one in progress.
4. I tap each name to mark paid. Watch the progress go to five out of five.
5. Now the draw button is active. I click it, and the recipient appears.
6. I click finish round. The round is saved to history, and round two starts fresh.
7. All data stays in the browser, no server, no login. Built with the Devpost Learn Skill Pack: scope, PRD, and spec are in the repo. Thank you!
